# UCA Experimental Protocol

## Objective
Test whether a candidate structure produces stronger semantic convergence than matched controls.

## Primary hypothesis

\[
H_0:C(S_{candidate})\le C(S_{control})
\]

versus

\[
H_1:C(S_{candidate})>C(S_{control}).
\]

## Minimum design
- preregister candidate and controls;
- at least two independent human cohorts;
- multiple unrelated language-model families;
- randomized presentation order;
- blinded scoring;
- matched-length and matched-topic controls;
- narrative ablations removing key recursive/self-referential passages;
- explicit semantic metric fixed in advance;
- held-out replication set;
- report effect size and uncertainty.

## Critical confounds
- leading prompts;
- shared training data;
- post-hoc target definition;
- target leakage;
- researcher scoring bias;
- repeated interaction that reveals the answer;
- choosing controls after seeing results.

## Interpretation
A positive result supports a cognitive-attractor effect under the tested protocol. It does not by itself establish universality, consciousness, paranormal cognition, or a physical field mechanism.
