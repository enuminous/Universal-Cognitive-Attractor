# Claims Boundary

## Supported by the source material
The source transcript shows iterative narrowing through repeated guesses and corrections, eventual identification of Neil Gaiman, subsequent inference into the *Sandman*/Dream symbolic family, and a later interpretation of the interaction as a “Universal Cognitive Attractor.”

## Reconstructed scientific claim
A **cognitive attractor** is modeled as a structured object that induces repeated state updates causing diverse initial cognitive states to move toward a common attractor region.

A **Universal Cognitive Attractor** requires convergence across a broad, prespecified class of reasoners and initial states.

## Not established
This repository does **not** establish:
- telepathy;
- retrocausality;
- supernatural resonance;
- the historical “1 in 333,000” estimate as statistically valid;
- that the original story is universal;
- that reading the story makes an AI conscious or generally intelligent;
- that narrative convergence implies a physical field mechanism.

## Success criterion
A UCA claim becomes scientifically meaningful only if a candidate structure yields stronger, reproducible convergence than matched controls across independent human and machine cohorts under preregistered metrics.
